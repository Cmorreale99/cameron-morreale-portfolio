# OneWorld — Tokenized Carbon Market Infrastructure

## Overview

Designed a blockchain-based carbon market infrastructure system that transforms emissions permits into programmable financial assets with transparent issuance, trading, and retirement logic.

Built the system around tokenized emissions permits, deterministic supply decay, tiered pricing, lifecycle constraints, and auditable on-chain market activity.

**Impact: Top 3 out of 100+ teams, demonstrating the ability to translate policy constraints, economic incentives, and environmental accountability into enforceable technical infrastructure.**

---

## Key Contributions

- Designed a **tokenized emissions permit system** where each OneWorldToken represents one metric ton of CO₂  
- Modeled emissions permits as **programmable financial assets** with enforceable lifecycle rules  
- Implemented a **deterministic supply decay model** of approximately 2.11% per quarter to align permit supply with long-term emissions reduction goals  
- Designed time-based constraints so permits could not be freely hoarded across periods to bypass future emissions limits  
- Created a **tiered pricing model** where the marginal cost of emissions increases as firms consume more permits  
- Embedded issuance, trading, and retirement logic into transparent blockchain infrastructure  
- Designed the architecture for real-time auditability of emissions behavior, permit movement, and market activity  
- Modeled integration with oracle systems to support future ingestion of real-world emissions data  

---

## Business Impact

- Placed **Top 3 out of 100+ teams** in a competitive blockchain hackathon  
- Translated cap-and-trade policy mechanics into programmable market infrastructure  
- Addressed common weaknesses in traditional carbon markets, including opacity, double-counting risk, weak enforcement, and susceptibility to manipulation  
- Created a system where emissions constraints are enforced by infrastructure rather than manual policy administration alone  
- Aligned economic incentives with environmental goals through supply decay, lifecycle restrictions, and tiered pricing  
- Demonstrated how blockchain infrastructure can improve transparency, auditability, and trust in regulated environmental markets

## Tech Stack

- **Blockchain:** Algorand  
- **Market Design:** Tokenized emissions permits, cap-and-trade mechanics, deterministic supply decay  
- **Economic Logic:** Tiered marginal pricing, emissions reduction incentives, anti-hoarding constraints  
- **Verification:** On-chain auditability, transparent permit lifecycle tracking  
- **Future Integration:** Oracle systems for real-world emissions data  
- **Focus Areas:** Carbon markets, programmable financial assets, market infrastructure, environmental accountability, blockchain-based audit systems
